Change of plans: the first building

The plan was always to start small — a cheap little cabin to feel out the waters. Then it was a lean-to, so rainwater catchment and solar could get going early. Now it's somewhere in the middle, and I think it's the right call.

The first building will be livable: about 300 square feet with a loft, a single-pitch roof, basic electric, and very basic plumbing — a sink, a shower, and a composting toilet. Off the side, an open covered bay for a workshop and tool storage.

The materials

  • Sheep wool insulation
  • Cypress tongue-and-groove siding
  • Cypress T&G inside too — no drywall
  • Pier-and-beam, stick-framed, nothing over 16 feet

What's still open

Where it goes. Access matters more than anything right now, since every board has to come up that logging road. A UTV and a small trailer might be the answer.
