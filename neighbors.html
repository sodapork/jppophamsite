No. 001Animals

Eastern Box Turtle

Terrapene carolina

Found
Fall 2026
Habitat
Leaf litter and woody debris on the forest floor

Cool fact: Can live for decades, often spending its whole life in a small home range.

No. 002Insects

Tree Cricket

Oecanthus sp.

Found
Fall 2026
Habitat
Tall weeds and shrubs

Cool fact: Males sing by rubbing those clear, harp-veined wings together. The snowy tree cricket chirps faster as it gets warmer, enough that you can estimate the temperature from it.

No. 003Insects

White Tiger Moth

Spilosoma sp. (ID tentative)

Found
Fall 2026
Habitat
Fields and woods edges; resting on a dry stem

Cool fact: Its caterpillars are the fuzzy "woolly bear" type you see crossing roads in the fall.

No. 004Plants

Heath Aster

Symphyotrichum ericoides (likely)

Found
Fall 2026
Habitat
Dry, open ground and old fields; blooms in fall

Cool fact: The centers start out yellow and turn pink to purple once a flower has been pollinated, so bees can skip the ones that are done.

No. 005Animals

Eastern Garter Snake

Thamnophis sirtalis

Found
Fall 2026
Habitat
Leaf litter and brushy edges

Cool fact: Harmless to people. Garter snakes stay active into colder weather than most snakes, and they often den up together in groups to wait out winter.
